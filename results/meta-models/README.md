# Meta models: what each one does, with and without it

One row per Meta model in the product. Numbers come from runs during the Meta hackathon (Oct 26 to Nov 4 2026), each with its date and its results folder. "Optional" means the product still works without it, and the row says how much worse.

| Meta model | What it does in the product | With it | Without it | Essential or optional | Results |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **SAM 3.1** (`facebook/sam3.1`, local on the Mac GPU) in the car seat coach | Finds the belt, belt guides, handle and level line by text, and the geometry checks the install from those masks | Test renders, Oct 28: 11 of 12 wrong installs flagged, 1 of 12 false alarms, 4 of 4 unseen frames abstained | Muse alone (image input), Oct 29: right on no twist 7 of 28, shoulder belt 12 of 28, lap belt 15 of 28. No other part of the product can check a belt | **Essential.** The coach is built on its masks | `results/carseat/test.md`, `results/carseat/muse/README.md` |
| **SAM 3.1** in the hover check (changing table, bath) | Its hand masks anchor the lift check that tells a hand 2 to 5 cm above the baby from a resting hand | Blind set, Oct 29: 23 of 32 hovers, 0 of 16 false voices (final design 22 of 32, 0 of 16) | Same lift check on MediaPipe regions: 21 of 32, 1 of 16 false voices. Reflex alone: 0 of 32 | **Optional, measurably better.** 2 more hovers and 1 fewer false voice on blind. The lift geometry does most of the work | `results/hover/blind/README.md`, `results/hover/wave2/README.md` |
| **SAM 3.1** confirm on the hand alarm | Confirms or cancels the reflex's hands-off alarm. On the changing table it now looks at a frame 0.8 s after the raise | Glasses feed, Oct 29: 8 of 8 hands-off verdicts right at 0.8 s (8 of 8 wrong on the raise frame). Hover dev set: table false voices 0 of 20 with the 0.8 s frame | Blind set with and without it on MediaPipe regions: 21 of 32 and 1 false voice both ways. The 1.2 s voice hold spoke before SAM 3.1 in every glasses run | **Optional.** It changes what the parent hears only on a near reading, where the voice waits for it | `results/glasses/wave2/README.md`, `results/hover/README.md` |
| **Muse Spark Open**, text (`meta-models/muse-spark-open-1026` on Baseten) | The brain of the family agent, the families bot and the demo: tool calls, answers, the baby log | Oct 28: 123 calls, 0 fallbacks, p50 1.32 s. Families 3-day Spanish 29 of 29 in 4 runs, demo visit 11 of 11 | Claude Haiku 5.5 as the brain: families 29, 28, 27 of 29, demo 11 of 11, 11 times cheaper | **Optional on quality.** It is the brain because it is Meta's open model, and it matched or beat Haiku | `results/muse/README.md` |
| **Muse Spark Open**, vision | Second opinion on the car seat: one zoomed still per step, a pass needs SAM 3.1 and Muse both | Test, Oct 29: 12 of 12 wrong installs not passed, 0 of 12 false fails, but 5 of 12 correct installs go to "unsure, check by hand", median 8.1 s a step | SAM 3.1 alone: 11 of 12, 1 false alarm | **Optional, opt-in** (`car_seat.muse`). Catches the one install SAM missed, and is too slow for the 2 s target | `results/carseat/muse/README.md` |
| **Muse Spark Open**, speech in | Hears a parent's Telegram voice note and writes it down for the brain | Oct 29: 20 of 20 notes logged right (10 Spanish, 10 English), median 7.55 s note to answer | No other ear in the product. Without it a voice note cannot be logged | **Essential for voice notes.** It hears speech, not house sounds: 11 of 46 house sound labels against 41 of 46 for PANNs, so the cry path does not use it | `results/voice/README.md`, `results/audio-truth/README.md`, `results/sounds/muse/README.md` |
| **MMS-TTS** (`facebook/mms-tts-spa`, `facebook/mms-tts-eng`, CC-BY-NC 4.0) | Speaks the answer back as a voice note | Oct 28 and 29: 20 of 20 answers sent as voice notes. Spoken back to Muse, the numbers survived in 18 of 20 | Any TTS would do. No other voice was compared | **Optional.** It is the voice because it is Meta's open model | `results/voice/README.md` |
| **Depth models**: DINOv2 + DPT (`facebook/dpt-dinov2-large-nyu`, `-giant-nyu`, Apache 2.0), Sapiens depth 1B (CC BY-NC 4.0) | Tried for the hover check: the hand's depth against the baby's | Oct 29: hover against touch AUC 0.41 to 0.66, chance is 0.5. The NYU models put the baby about 1.2 m away, not 0.45 m | The lift check, which measures perspective growth instead | **Not used.** They failed at this job | `results/hover/lift/README.md` (`depth.json`) |
| **DAT** (Meta Wearables Device Access Toolkit, MockDeviceKit 1.0.1) | Streams the Ray-Ban Meta camera into the companion app, which posts frames to the agent | iOS simulator with a mock Ray-Ban Meta, Oct 28: 10 fps, hands off spoken 5 of 5, 0 false alarms. With SAM 3.1 on, Oct 29: 4 of 4 | The phone on a head strap through the head-camera page, the same skill | **Essential for the glasses path, untested on real glasses.** No real pairing yet | `results/glasses/README.md` |

## Where each one is optional, plainly

- SAM 3.1 is essential in the car seat coach and only helpful in the hover check, where it adds 2 hovers and removes 1 false voice on a 48-moment blind set.
- Muse Spark Open is essential as the ear for voice notes. As the text brain it is a choice: Haiku did about as well for less.
- MMS-TTS and the Muse vision second opinion are choices, not needs.
- The depth models did not work for the hover. Nothing in the product uses them.
- Every camera number above is on rendered clips. None is from a real baby or a real parent's hand.
