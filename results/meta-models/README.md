# Meta models: what each one does, with and without it

One row per Meta model in the product. Numbers come from runs during the Meta hackathon (Oct 26 to Nov 4 2026), each with its date and its results folder. "Optional" means the product still works without it, and the row says how much worse.

| Meta model | What it does in the product | With it | Without it | Essential or optional | Results |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

No other part of the product can check a belt | **Essential.** The coach is built on its masks | `results/carseat/test.md`, `results/carseat/muse/README.md` |
| **SAM 3.1** in the dog watch | Finds "dog", "baby", "bassinet" and "play mat" in a living room camera frame. ## Where each one is optional, plainly

- SAM 3.1 is essential in the car seat coach, the dog watch and the held object hover, and only helpful in the empty-hand hover check, where it adds 2 hovers and removes 1 false voice on a 48-moment blind set.
- Muse Spark Open is essential as the ear for voice notes. As the text brain it is a choice: Haiku did about as well for less. It proposes the families log operations, the design that beat the rule guards 1 to 13 of 45, but no other model was tried on that design. It plans the house and wins only the 3 adaptation cases, the fixed rules matched it on the day.
- MMS-TTS and the Muse vision second opinion are choices, not needs.
- The depth models did not work for the hover. Nothing in the product uses them.
- Every camera number above is on rendered clips, except the dog watch, which is on Wikimedia Commons photos and composites. None is from a real baby on our camera or a real parent's hand.
