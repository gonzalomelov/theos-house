# What the agent may do in the house

### Alone (low risk, always read back)

- Study light: on, off, dim, warm for night feeds.
- Echo Dot: do not disturb on during a skill, off after. Announce or speak at low volume.
- TVs: mute and unmute.
- Vacuum: pause, return to base during a nap or a skill.
- Gonza's iPhone: a push notification.
- To-do and shopping lists: add an item ("diapers").

### Only with Gonza's yes

- Anything outside the house: outdoor floodlight, outdoor camera.
- Camera floodlights and IR lamps.
- Starting the vacuum while someone is home, unless a parent asks for it directly.
- TV power, volume above a low level, changing what plays.
- Any automation.

### Never

- Camera privacy switches and recording settings, sirens, tamper and alarm triggers.
- The bathroom Shelly plug (`switch.shellyplusplugit_bathroom`). It has its own safety cut automation.
- The refrigerator settings, backups, updates, the body scale.
- Anything that affects neighbours or other people's devices.
