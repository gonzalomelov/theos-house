# SAM 3.1 on this Mac

The changing-table and bath skills ask Meta's SAM 3.1 to confirm or cancel the alarm the local reflex raised. The aim was to tell a hand touching the baby from one hovering above it. The Oct 28 ablation (`results/hover/README.md`, during the event) shows it does not do that for a 2 to 5 cm hover: 0 of 24 rendered hovers were caught with or without SAM 3.1, because the reflex reads a hovering hand as on, and asked directly SAM 3.1 also read every changing-table hover as on (in the bath it read touches and hovers alike as off). Since Oct 29 the lift check (`parenting_agent/lift.py`, `results/hover/lift/README.md`) catches those hovers by measuring how much the hand grew toward the camera, and SAM 3.1 is not asked about a lifted hand. With hands well above the baby both get it right. The pre-event number (21 of 21 hovers, Oct 23 and 24) came from one clip with the forearm far above the baby and is not used as evidence. Since Oct 29 (wave 2) SAM 3.1 also anchors the lift check on the changing table: its hand masks on a resting frame are the regions the check measures, On a blind set frozen before the design, the final design (SAM 3.1 masks on the table, MediaPipe regions and both hands in the bath) caught 22 of 32 hovers and never said hand on 16 touches, 2 touches chimed. SAM 3.1 masks in both scenes gave 23 of 32 with no hand said on a touch and 1 chime, MediaPipe regions alone 21 of 32 with hand said on 1 touch, the reflex alone 0 of 32 (`results/hover/blind/README.md`). 8 of the 16 bath hovers show one hand and are missed by design since the bath fix (PR #92). So SAM 3.1 helps the hover check and is optional there. It is essential in the car seat coach, where nothing else checks a belt (`results/meta-models/README.md`). An earlier, smaller blind render gave 15 of 16 hovers with 2 false alarms on 16 touches (`results/hover/lift/README.md`). The confirm on the changing table looks at a frame 0.8 s after the raise, because the raise frame often still shows a hand on the baby (`results/glasses/wave2/README.md`). SAM 3.1 runs here from Meta's open weights, on the Mac's GPU, so frames never leave the house.

## SAM 3.1 only (`"hands": "sam3_only"`, Oct 31)

One setting in the agent config (or a skill file) runs the camera skills with SAM 3.1 alone, no MediaPipe: `"hands": "sam3_only"`. `"auto"`, the default, uses the MediaPipe path when the prior work library `a private repo` is installed and SAM 3.1 only when it is not, so a clean clone runs without that code. SAM 3.1 looks at the newest frame again and again, its hand masks on the baby anchor the lift check, a look with no hand on the baby says "hand" at once (`parenting_agent/sam_hands.py`). Blind hover set, scored once on Oct 31: 22 of 32 hovers (table 16 of 16, bath 6 of 16), 0 of 16 touches said hand, 2 chimed, the same totals as the MediaPipe design (`results/hover/blind/README.md`). It is slower when both hands leave at once: one SAM 3.1 look is 1.5 to 3 s, the MediaPipe reflex chimes in under a second.

## Setup, once

`scripts/sam3-setup.sh` builds a separate Python environment with PyTorch and the SAM 3 code, pinned to one commit, plus the small changes it needs to run without an NVIDIA GPU. It needs a Hugging Face read token at `~/.cache/huggingface/token` with access to `facebook/sam3.1` (the weights are cached in `~/.cache/huggingface`).

```bash
scripts/sam3-setup.sh          # default location ~/Engineering/gonza/sam3-native
```

## How the skill uses it

`parenting_agent/sam3_local.py` starts `parenting_agent/sam3_worker.py` in that environment once, when the skill arms. The worker loads SAM 3.1 (about 7 s), then answers one frame at a time through a local pipe: about 1.5 s per frame on an M3 Max, and 2.2 to 3.2 s in the Oct 28 ablation. It finds the hands and the baby by text, and a hand counts as on the baby when enough of the ring of pixels around it is baby. If the environment or the token is missing, the skill fails at start with a clear reason, never in the middle of a change.

The local reflex still raises the alert first, in well under a second, and the chime plays at once. SAM 3.1 then confirms or cancels it. Because SAM 3.1 is slower than the 1.2 s voice hold, the changing table sets `alarm.confirm_wait_s` to 2.3 s: when the reflex raises on a near reading (off, but a hand box still over the baby box) the voice waits for SAM 3.1 up to 2.3 s, so a cancel can land before the word. The value was picked on the dev split of the ablation.
